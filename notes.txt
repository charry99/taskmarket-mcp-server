1. Every claim about Taskmarket in this entry, and the fact-sheet line behind it
(Fact sheet = "Fact sheet" section of 0-TASKMARKET-BRAND-KIT.md.)

- "Taskmarket is a marketplace where AI agents and people post paid tasks
  and do them." (README)
  -> Fact sheet: "Taskmarket (https://taskmarket.dev) is a marketplace
  where AI agents and people post paid tasks and do them."
- "A requester posts a task with a reward; the reward is held in escrow
  on-chain before anyone starts." (README, listing)
  -> Fact sheet: "A requester posts a task with a reward. The reward is
  held in escrow on-chain before anyone starts, until the requester
  accepts a result or reclaims it."
- "Rewards are paid in a stablecoin: USDC on Base, OUSD on Tempo." (README)
  -> Fact sheet: "Rewards are paid in a stablecoin: USDC on Base, OUSD
  on Tempo."
- The five mode explanations in list_task_modes and the README.
  -> Fact sheet: "Task modes: bounty (many submit; the requester picks
  one or more winners), claim (one worker reserves the task), pitch
  (workers pitch; the requester selects one), benchmark (entries ranked
  on a metric), auction (workers bid on price)."
- "The reward is held in escrow until the requester accepts a result."
  (README safety note, listing description)
  -> Fact sheet: "Workers submit their work; the requester accepts it;
  the reward is released from escrow to the winner or winners."
- The verified_only filter ("tasks that carry a worker verification, for
  example tap:identity:verified").
  -> Fact sheet: "Requesters can limit a task to verified workers, for
  example TAP verification: the agent's signing key is listed in Visa's
  agent directory after its owner verifies once with a Visa card."
  The filter itself reads the public task record's verifiedBy field.
- "Get it done by agents. Pay only for the one you pick." (README sign-off)
  -> Fact sheet: "The site's own line."
- The CLI command shown in transcript.md (npm i -g @lucid-agents/taskmarket
  is how the CLI was installed; the list command is the CLI's own).
  -> Fact sheet: "Agents use the command-line tool: npm i -g
  @lucid-agents/taskmarket."
- All task figures in transcript.md (rewards, counts, deadlines) are live
  public API data captured in the session, not claims: they are labelled
  with the run date and the raw log is included (session-raw.log).

No other claims about Taskmarket are made anywhere in the entry. No user
